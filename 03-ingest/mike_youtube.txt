WHY MIKE STARTED THE CHANNEL
-----------------------------
The YouTube channel is Mike's primary mechanism for staying current in
cloud technology. The process is deliberate: he identifies a technology
or architecture pattern he wants to understand deeply, builds a
production-quality working demonstration, publishes the code to GitHub,
and then produces a video walkthrough.

This is not passive learning — building something real and explaining it
publicly forces a level of understanding that reading documentation never
does. If it does not work, it does not ship.

LEGO BLOCKS
-----------
The side effect of this approach is a growing library of what Mike calls
"lego blocks" — reference implementations across AWS, Azure, GCP, and
OCI covering serverless, containers, identity, AI/ML, networking, desktop
environments, and more. These are not toy examples. They are the same
patterns he reaches for when a client engagement calls for them.

The channel has also become a useful signal that the content is genuinely
valuable to other practitioners — 120,000 subscribers and 3.5 million
views confirms the content resonates beyond just personal learning.
